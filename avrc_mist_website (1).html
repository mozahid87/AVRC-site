About AVRC

The Advanced VLSI Research Centre (AVRC) at MIST is a dedicated research laboratory under the Department of Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering (EECE), focused on cutting-edge Very Large Scale Integration research and development.

Our centre bridges theoretical fundamentals and real-world semiconductor design, training future engineers in chip design, verification, and fabrication methodologies in alignment with global industry standards.

AVRC fosters collaboration between faculty, graduate researchers, and industry partners to drive innovation in next-generation integrated circuit technologies for Bangladesh and beyond.

// 02 · Focus Areas

Research Areas

⚡

Low Power CMOS Design

Investigating ultra-low-power circuit topologies and dynamic voltage scaling techniques for energy-efficient integrated systems.

🔲

Digital VLSI & RTL Design

Developing complex digital systems using HDL-based RTL design methodologies, synthesis, and physical layout verification flows.

📡

RF & Analog IC Design

Research into high-frequency analog circuits, amplifiers, oscillators, and mixed-signal systems for wireless communications.

🔧

FPGA Prototyping

Rapid FPGA-based prototyping of ASIC designs for early hardware validation and accelerated design iteration cycles.

🛡️

Hardware Security

Exploring hardware Trojans, side-channel attacks, physically unclonable functions (PUFs), and secure chip design methodologies.

🧠

AI Hardware Accelerators

Designing dedicated silicon architectures for neural network inference, including in-memory computing and neuromorphic circuits.
